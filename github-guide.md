        <h3>Make your GitHub account</h3>
        <p>Go to <strong>github.com</strong> and click Sign up. Pick a username, enter your email, choose a password, and verify the email. Nothing to install.</p>
        <p>Optional: click the green <strong>New</strong> button to create your first repo (your first project folder). You can skip this for now and come back to it.</p>

        <p><strong>The builder way (let Claude work in your code):</strong> go to <strong>claude.ai/code</strong>, sign in with your GitHub account, pick a repo, and give it a task.</p>

<section class="advance-intro">
  <div class="container">
    <h2>The words you will hear</h2>
    <p><strong>Repo:</strong> the folder for one project.</p>
    <p><strong>Commit:</strong> save a snapshot, with a little note about what changed.</p>
    <p><strong>Push:</strong> send that snapshot up to the cloud.</p>
    <p><strong>Clone:</strong> copy a project down to your own computer.</p>
    <p><strong>Fork:</strong> your own copy of someone else's repo, so you can build on it without touching theirs.</p>
    <p class="advance-quote">One setting to know when you make a repo: Public means anyone can find it, Private means just you and who you invite. Not sharing it with the world? Choose Private. You can change it later.</p>
  </div>
</section>
