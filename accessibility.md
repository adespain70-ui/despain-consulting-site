*Last reviewed: September 2026*

We want everyone to be able to use despain-consulting.com, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.

## Our Standard

We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. In practice, that means:

- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link
- Text and buttons meet WCAG color contrast requirements
- Images have text alternatives, and pages use a clear heading structure and page landmarks
- Pages reflow to a 320-pixel-wide screen without horizontal scrolling
- Animation and smooth scrolling are turned off when your device asks for reduced motion
- Links that open a new tab say so

## How We Test

We check the site with automated WCAG 2.2 AA testing and by hand, including keyboard-only navigation and mobile screen sizes. We review accessibility again whenever we make significant changes to the site.

## Third-Party Tools

Some parts of the site are provided by other companies, and we don't control their code:

- **Newsletter and guide sign-up forms** are provided by Kit
- **Booking and scheduling** is provided by zcal, with payment processed by Stripe

If one of these doesn't work for you, contact us and we'll help you sign up or book another way.

## Feedback and Help

If you run into a barrier on this site, or need information in a different format, please tell us. Email [anne@despain-consulting.com](mailto:anne@despain-consulting.com) with the page and what happened. We respond within 1-2 business days.
