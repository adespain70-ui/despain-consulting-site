# DeSpain Consulting Visual Brief

Use this recipe for the default visual identity. Copy the layout and design
language; ignore the sample document's subject matter. The look is warm, light,
and editorial: a cream page with a warm-brown frame, a serif title, and clean
sans-serif supporting copy. It carries large-company rigor without noise.

## Page composition

- One A4 portrait page for a client one-pager or decision brief; use A3 only
  when a dense operating model stays readable at A4.
- Cream page. A cream header keeps the warm-brown DeSpain wordmark legible; the
  footer sits in a warm-brown band with cream text.
- DeSpain Consulting wordmark at top left on the cream header; small taupe
  uppercase eyebrow and warm-brown category at top right.
- A thin warm-brown rule below the header.
- Centered title in the serif face, then version/date/prepared-for metadata,
  then a one-to-three sentence plain-language summary.
- One large rounded cream-white diagram panel as the main content, framed by a
  thin taupe stroke.
- Footer: `DESPAIN CONSULTING`, a short principle, `despain-consulting.com`,
  and page number.

## Palette

Lead with warm brown and cream. The neutral structure stays in the brand
browns and taupes; a small set of restrained functional accents separates the
states that carry decision, proof, or risk meaning. Always pair an accent with
its status word or a legend, never color alone.

Set titles and the wordmark in Playfair Display (serif). Set the summary,
metadata, footer, and all diagram node text in Inter (sans-serif) for
readability at small sizes. Both are Google Fonts and are licensed for
embedding. Keep structural connectors thin and `#A59E97`.

## Diagram character

- One top-to-bottom primary path.
- Short nearby branches that rejoin close to their origin.
- Named decisions, holds, evidence gates, and terminal outcomes.
- Two or three short lines per node; no decorative nodes or unexplained color.
- Preserve uncertainty and disagreement instead of smoothing them into success.
- Lead with the reader's outcome. Keep the method secondary and state the
  result the page proves, leaving the tooling out of the headline.

## Acceptance

The page must read clearly at normal zoom, keep every node inside the diagram
panel, show the correct title and metadata, use the cream/warm-brown identity
with the DeSpain wordmark, contain no black squares or broken glyphs, and
contain no em dash characters.
