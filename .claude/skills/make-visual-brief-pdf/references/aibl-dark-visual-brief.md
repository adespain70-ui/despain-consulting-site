# AIBL Dark Student Visual Brief

Use this recipe for the default visual identity. Copy the layout and design
language, not the sample document's subject matter.

## Page composition

- One A3 portrait page for a detailed operating model; use A4 only when the
  diagram remains readable.
- Charcoal page with a near-black header and footer.
- AIBL wordmark at top left; small white eyebrow and lime category at top
  right.
- Thin lime rules below the header and above the footer.
- Centered title, then version/date/preparer metadata, then a one-to-three
  sentence plain-language summary.
- One large rounded charcoal diagram panel as the main content.
- Footer: `AI BUILD LAB`, a short principle, URL, and page number.

## Diagram character

- One top-to-bottom primary path.
- Short nearby branches that rejoin close to their origin.
- Named decisions, holds, evidence gates, and terminal outcomes.
- Two or three short lines per node; no decorative nodes or unexplained color.
- Preserve uncertainty and disagreement instead of smoothing them into success.

## Acceptance

The page must read clearly at normal zoom, keep every node inside the diagram
panel, show correct title and metadata, contain no black squares or broken
glyphs, and contain no em dash characters.
