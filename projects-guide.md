<section class="advance-intro">
  <div class="container">
    <p class="advance-quote">"Projects used to be folders. Now each one is a conversation that splits into threads."</p>
    <p>In the <strong>Claude Code desktop app</strong>, a project is now one ongoing chat. You ask for things in the <strong>main chat</strong>, and Claude starts <strong>threads</strong> that work in parallel and report back. Every thread reads the same project notes, so an answer you give in one place reaches the rest.</p>
    <p>I tested it on my gift business's holiday prep: a gift guide, a customer email, a social calendar, and a shipping FAQ. You do not need to write code. You do need the Claude Code desktop app and a clear goal.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="advance-intro">
  <div class="container">
    <h2>What you need</h2>
    <p><strong>The Claude Code desktop app</strong>, signed in to a paid Claude plan. Projects is in beta and rolling out by plan, so if you don't see it in your sidebar, check what your plan includes.</p>
    <p><strong>One real project with a finish line.</strong> Holiday prep, a launch, a newsletter series. Something with three or four pieces that depend on the same facts.</p>
    <p><strong>A short context file</strong> about your business (template in step 3).</p>
    <p><strong>Optional:</strong> Google Drive connected to your Claude account, if your product lists or notes live there.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="advance-framework">
  <div class="container">

    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">1</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Clean house, then upgrade</h2>
        <p>In the Claude desktop app, switch to the <strong>Code</strong> tab (the <strong>&lt;/&gt;</strong> button at the top), then click <strong>Projects</strong> in the sidebar. It is marked Beta. Click <strong>Get started</strong>. Before it lets you in, it asks you to upgrade your existing projects or archive them. I had seven. I archived the four I'm done with and upgraded three. The upgrade runs in the background, so you can close the window and keep working.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">2</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Fill in the New project form</h2>
        <p>Click <strong>Create new project</strong>. There are three fields: <strong>Name</strong>, <strong>Goal</strong>, and <strong>Context</strong>. The examples in the form are written for developers, but nothing in it needs code or a repository.</p><p>The Goal is the most important line you will write. Name the finished pieces you want, so Claude knows what done looks like. Mine:</p><div style="background:#F6F1EB;border:1px solid #D9CEC3;border-left:4px solid #877167;border-radius:8px;padding:14px 18px;margin:12px 0;font-family:'SFMono-Regular',Menlo,Consolas,monospace;font-size:0.9rem;line-height:1.55;color:#3f342f;white-space:pre-wrap;"><span style="display:block;font-family:Inter,sans-serif;font-size:0.72rem;font-weight:600;letter-spacing:0.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#736962;margin-bottom:6px;">Goal field</span>Get ahead of the holiday rush with a gift guide, a customer email, a social calendar, and a shipping and delivery cutoff FAQ, all in my voice.</div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">3</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Add a short context file</h2>
        <p>Under Context, click <strong>+ Add</strong>. You can add a file, a folder, a repository, or a <strong>Google Drive folder</strong>. I wrote a short file about my business and added it as a file. Here is the template (fill in the brackets and save it as a text or Markdown file):</p><div style="background:#F6F1EB;border:1px solid #D9CEC3;border-left:4px solid #877167;border-radius:8px;padding:14px 18px;margin:12px 0;font-family:'SFMono-Regular',Menlo,Consolas,monospace;font-size:0.9rem;line-height:1.55;color:#3f342f;white-space:pre-wrap;"><span style="display:block;font-family:Inter,sans-serif;font-size:0.72rem;font-weight:600;letter-spacing:0.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#736962;margin-bottom:6px;">Context file template</span>[Business name] is my [what you do] in [where]. I [what you sell and how it gets made]. [How it reaches customers: shipping, delivery, pickup]. [Your busiest season or biggest customer group].

Write in my voice: [three or four words that describe how you sound]. [Anything you never want in your writing]. Never invent prices, dates, or product details. Ask me when you need one.</div><p>Keep the last line. It is the reason my project asked me for dates and prices instead of making them up.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">4</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Let it suggest the threads</h2>
        <p>Click <strong>Create project</strong> and wait. Before I typed anything, Claude read my goal and context file and came back with four suggested threads: draft the gift guide, write the customer email, plan the social calendar, and write the shipping cutoff FAQ. Each had a one-line reason. It also told me it used my recent Claude sessions, so know that it can see those.</p><p>One click on <strong>Start 4 threads</strong> and all four ran at once. The gift guide thread searched my Google Drive, found my basket contents sheet, and built the guide from my real products. It left every price blank because it could not confirm my old prices were still current.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">5</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Answer once, in the thread it points you to</h2>
        <p>Three threads needed my shipping dates. The main chat noticed and told me to answer once, in the FAQ thread, and it would pass my dates to the other two. Rough answers are fine. Mine:</p><div style="background:#F6F1EB;border:1px solid #D9CEC3;border-left:4px solid #877167;border-radius:8px;padding:14px 18px;margin:12px 0;font-family:'SFMono-Regular',Menlo,Consolas,monospace;font-size:0.9rem;line-height:1.55;color:#3f342f;white-space:pre-wrap;"><span style="display:block;font-family:Inter,sans-serif;font-size:0.72rem;font-weight:600;letter-spacing:0.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#736962;margin-bottom:6px;">Type in the thread</span>1. I ship with USPS, UPS, and FedEx. I don't offer an expedited or express option.
2. During the holidays I need 2 to 3 business days to build a basket before it goes to the carrier.
3. Last day to order for shipped delivery by Christmas is Monday, December 14. Corporate and bulk orders need to be in by Friday, November 13, about a month before that cutoff, so I have time to source from my Utah makers.
4. I hand-deliver in Park City, nearby towns like Kamas and Heber, and Salt Lake City. Last day to order for hand delivery is Tuesday, December 22, and my last delivery day is Thursday, December 24.
5. The studio is closed December 25 through 27. No international shipping. No perishables note needed this season.</div><p>The main chat confirmed it sent my dates to the email and social calendar threads. Then the FAQ thread caught a problem in my own dates. A December 14 order that takes three days to build ships December 17, the same day as the USPS Ground Advantage send-by date, which leaves no buffer for coast-to-coast ground.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">6</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Steer a thread when your plans change</h2>
        <p>Every thread has its own box at the bottom that says <strong>Steer this thread</strong>. I used it to split my cutoff and asked it to pass the change along:</p><div style="background:#F6F1EB;border:1px solid #D9CEC3;border-left:4px solid #877167;border-radius:8px;padding:14px 18px;margin:12px 0;font-family:'SFMono-Regular',Menlo,Consolas,monospace;font-size:0.9rem;line-height:1.55;color:#3f342f;white-space:pre-wrap;"><span style="display:block;font-family:Inter,sans-serif;font-size:0.72rem;font-weight:600;letter-spacing:0.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#736962;margin-bottom:6px;">Type in the thread</span>Let's split the shipping cutoff. Keep Monday, December 14 as the last day to order for delivery in Utah and nearby states (Idaho, Colorado, Wyoming, Nevada, Arizona). For everywhere else in the US, the last day to order is Friday, December 11, so there's a buffer before the carrier deadlines. Please update the FAQ, and send the new date to the email and social calendar threads too.</div><p>It updated the FAQ, saved the new dates to the project notes, sent them to the other two threads, and checked that December 11 is a Friday.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">7</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Send project-wide changes to the main chat</h2>
        <p>When a change touches several threads, type it in the main chat instead of a single thread. I raised my price floor halfway through:</p><div style="background:#F6F1EB;border:1px solid #D9CEC3;border-left:4px solid #877167;border-radius:8px;padding:14px 18px;margin:12px 0;font-family:'SFMono-Regular',Menlo,Consolas,monospace;font-size:0.9rem;line-height:1.55;color:#3f342f;white-space:pre-wrap;"><span style="display:block;font-family:Inter,sans-serif;font-size:0.72rem;font-weight:600;letter-spacing:0.08em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#736962;margin-bottom:6px;">Type in the main chat</span>My prices changed: $88 is now my lowest price, and the bands are $88 to $128, $138 to $158, and $168 and up. Check the email and social calendar threads for the old $38 and $78 prices and update them.</div><p>It sent the change to both threads and told me up front there might be nothing to fix, since both drafts were written without prices. Both came back clean, and it offered one useful addition instead of busywork.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">8</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Edit the file yourself and hand it back</h2>
        <p>You do not have to describe every change in chat. I opened the gift guide draft, made my edits and notes right in the file, attached it back to the thread, and typed <strong>"updated guide"</strong>. The thread read my edits, summarized what changed, and asked four short follow-up questions. Shorthand answers worked fine.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="advance-step">
      <div class="advance-letter">9</div>
      <div class="advance-step-content">
        <h2>Check the Overview</h2>
        <p>Click <strong>Overview</strong> at the top to see every thread grouped by status: <strong>Waiting on you</strong>, <strong>Idle</strong>, and <strong>Resolved</strong>. A thread that wrote something it wants you to check stays open until you OK it. It will not mark itself done on work that needs your eyes.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

<section class="advance-intro">
  <div class="container">
    <h2>Settings worth knowing</h2>
    <p>Click the gear next to Overview.</p>
    <p><strong>Memory:</strong> this is how the routing works. <strong>Project instructions</strong> work like a rulebook every new thread follows. Claude wrote mine on its own, and you can edit them. Below that, Claude keeps its own notes files that every thread reads, like my makers list and shipping dates. To change a note, tell it in plain English in the box at the bottom.</p>
    <p><strong>Environment:</strong> all of your claude.ai connectors are available in every thread, which is how mine reached Google Drive.</p>
    <p><strong>General:</strong> you can edit the goal, see the models (both the main chat and the threads ran Opus 5.5 in my project), turn on auto-continue so threads pick up when usage limits reset, and pause the project.</p>
  </div>
</section>

The app also gives up to <strong>$100 of initial usage</strong>, including the automatic setup, that does not count toward your usage limits, and my setup ran inside it.
